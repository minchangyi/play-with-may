Privacy Policy — Play with May

Effective 21 August 2026

Play with May is made by Min Chang Yi. This policy describes what the app does with information. It is short because the app does very little.

What the app collects

Nothing.

There is no account, no sign-up, no email address, no password, and no profile. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party software development kits of any kind.

The app makes no network requests of its own. It does not talk to a server belonging to the developer, because no such server exists.

What the app keeps

Everything the app remembers is stored on your own device:

  • the name you gave your companion
  • which stages you have finished, and the grid of any stage you left part-solved
  • the days you played, and your longest run of days
  • the ink you have earned and the items you own
  • how you have arranged your playroom and dressed your companion
  • your app settings, including a reminder time if you chose one

What the app never records

  • How long a stage took you, in any form you or anyone else can see. Elapsed time is used once, to decide that a finished grid was actually solved rather than tapped through, and is then shown to nobody.
  • Mistakes. A wrongly filled square is not counted, flagged, scored or stored. There is no error count anywhere in the app.
  • Hints. They are free, unlimited, and uncounted.
  • Days you missed. There is no "days since" counter, because nothing in the app needs one.

iCloud

The app uses CloudKit, which means that if you are signed in to iCloud your progress syncs through your own private iCloud database.

That copy belongs to you. It sits inside your personal Apple account, under Apple's privacy terms, and the developer has no access to it and no way to read it. Turning iCloud off in iOS Settings stops this; the app then keeps everything on the device and needs no network at all.

Notifications

The app can send you one reminder a day, at a time you choose.

It asks iOS for notification permission only after you have chosen a reminder time — the prompt follows your intent, it never leads it. If you never choose a time, the app never asks.

These are local notifications, scheduled on your device by your device. Nothing about them leaves your phone, and there is no push server involved. The wording is always an invitation and never says you are behind.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no purchases, no links out to the web, no chat, and no way for one person using the app to reach another.

Your puzzles are yours

There is no export, because nothing is held anywhere but your device and your own iCloud. Deleting the app deletes what it stored locally; you can remove its iCloud data from iOS Settings.
