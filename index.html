Quiet picture puzzles. Solve the picture, and the picture is yours to keep.

Getting help

There is no account to recover and no password to reset, so most questions are quick ones. Write to minchangyi@me.com and you will get a reply from the person who made the app.

Your data

The app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

If something is broken, it helps to say which iPhone and which version of iOS you are on, and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots are welcome.

If something in the app made you feel judged, chased, or behind, that is a design failure and worth an email — the whole app is built to avoid exactly that.
